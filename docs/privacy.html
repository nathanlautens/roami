roami. privacy policy

Last updated 16 October 2026

Roami helps you make plans with friends. This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. Short version: we keep only what the app needs to work, we never sell your data, and you can delete everything at any time.

What we collect

  • Your email address, to create your account and send your sign-in code.
  • Your profile: the display name and optional photo you choose. Your friends on Roami can see these.
  • Your friends and circles: who you're friends with, and the circles you make. Circles are private to you.
  • Your plans: Roams you send, your answers (In, Maybe, Can't), notes, votes, and ideas. These are visible to the people on that Roam, following the rules in the app (for example, only the host sees who said Maybe or Can't).
  • A push notification token, if you allow notifications, so we can tell you when friends are free.

Face ID and fingerprint unlock happen entirely on your phone. Roami never sees or stores your face or fingerprint data.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, no selling or sharing your data with advertisers.
  • We don't read your contacts or your location.

Where your data lives

Roami runs on Supabase, which stores the data securely on our behalf. Sign-in emails are sent by our email provider. Push notifications go through Expo, Apple and Google. These providers only process data to run the service.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to your profile and tap Delete my account. This permanently removes your account, profile, photo, friendships, circles, and every Roam you've hosted or answered. You can also email us and we'll do it for you.

Kids

Roami isn't intended for children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
